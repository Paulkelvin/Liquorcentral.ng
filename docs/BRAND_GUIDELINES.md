# Brand Guidelines

**Status: Partially populated.** `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` (v1, drafted 2026-07-18) now defines the brand's personality, philosophy, emotional direction, and visual foundation — **read that document first.** This document's role has narrowed to what `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` explicitly leaves open: concrete, asset-level execution that doesn't exist yet.

> **Relationship to `BRAND_IDENTITY.md`:** the two documents coexist with distinct responsibilities and neither supersedes the other. `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` is the durable, strategic *why* — personality, voice, emotional goals, positioning, and visual/verbal principles. This document is the applied, tactical *how* — the actual logo files, the specific chosen typefaces, the finished photography library, a bank of real tone-of-voice copy examples. See `BRAND_IDENTITY.md`'s own reconciliation note (near the top of that file) for the full comparison table.

> Do not invent logos, exact color hex values (the four brand colors are already fixed — see `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §13), specific typefaces, taglines, or specific tone-of-voice copy on behalf of this document. If a decision is needed before it's made here, flag it in `PROJECT_STATUS.md` rather than assuming an answer.

## What is decided (defined in `BRAND_IDENTITY.md`, referenced here)

- Brand vision, mission, core values, personality, voice, tone, emotional and perception goals — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §1–8.
- The brand is **one cohesive LiquorCentral ecosystem**, with Food Central presented as a premium subsidiary, not a separate brand or website — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §21, `PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md` §2.
- The four approved brand colors and their usage hierarchy (Off White dominant; Red as sparing action accent; Green as functional/affirmative accent; Gold as rare ceremonial accent) — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §13. **The colors themselves (hex values) are fixed and out of scope for any future revision without a new decision.**
- Typography *direction* (warmth for headings, legibility for body) — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §14. The specific typeface is not yet chosen — see below.
- Photography, art direction, motion, iconography, illustration, and white-space principles — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §15–20.
- Content tone should be consistent across both product lines (one voice, two subjects) — `PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md` §12, `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §5–6.

## What is not yet decided (this document's remaining scope)

- **Logo and wordmark** — no logo exists yet; a formal logo process is future work briefed from `BRAND_IDENTITY.md`, not decided within it.
- **The specific typeface family** for headings and body — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §14 sets direction (warm/confident display face, highly legible body face) but does not name one; selection (including licensing/webfont evaluation) belongs to the Design System phase.
- **The finished photography library** — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §15 sets the shot-list and grading standard; actual photographs don't exist yet.
- **A tone-of-voice example bank** — real, reviewed copy examples per context (discovery, transactional, compliance, celebratory, recovery — see `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §6's tone matrix), to be built up as real copy is written.
- **The real brand story** — `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §9 defines the *shape* the story should take; the specific narrative facts (founding, milestones) are Paul's to provide.
- Logo lockups, favicon, social/marketing assets, packaging or delivery-branding (rider uniforms, bags, receipts).
- **How Food Central is visually distinguished** from Wine & Spirits at the asset level (e.g. specific iconography or imagery choices within the shared system) — while still reading as one ecosystem, per `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` §21/24.

## How this document should be filled in

As each item above is produced (by Paul, or a designer engaged for this purpose), it should be added here in finished, asset-level form:

1. The real brand story, once Paul provides it.
2. Logo files and usage rules.
3. The chosen typeface family, weights, and licensing.
4. A finished, curated photography library reference.
5. A voice/tone example bank with real copy for both Wine & Spirits and Food Central.
6. Any asset-level Food Central visual distinction within the shared system.

Each addition should be logged in `DECISION_LOG.md` and reflected in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`. None of this work should begin before `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` v1 is approved by Paul.
