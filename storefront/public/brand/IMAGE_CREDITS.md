## What was done to `hero-food-drink.jpg`

The generated file's background was a flat `#E5E7D7` — uniform across all four
corners, but darker and greener than the page's `#F3F5F0`, so it would have
shown as a visible rectangle.

Rather than disguise that at runtime with a blend mode and filters (which the
previous stand-in needed, at the cost of washing out the product and erasing
its shadow), the correction was **baked into the file**: a per-channel gain of
`1.061 / 1.058 / 1.116` maps the ground exactly onto `#F3F5F0`, verified
afterwards at every corner as within ±2 (JPEG noise, invisible). The
generator's decorative sparkle in the lower right was also painted out, touching
only pixels *brighter* than the ground so the wooden board and the contact
shadow were left intact.

**Consequence for the component:** the hero needs no `mix-blend-mode`, no
filter, no mask and no synthetic shadow. Keep it that way — match any
replacement image's background to the section colour instead.

## Known defect in the current image

⚠️ **The contact shadow is clipped on the left edge.** Measured on the file:
the extreme left column carries dark pixels from roughly y840–984 (darkest 196
against a 243 ground), where the shadow runs out of frame and terminates in a
straight vertical line. The right and bottom edges are clean.

This is baked into the source image — no CSS can repair a shadow that is
already cut. It is a source-image problem to fix on the next generation, and
`HERO_IMAGE_BRIEF.md` now carries explicit rules requiring every object *and
its shadow* to sit fully inside the frame with clear margins.

A second, related request is open: the composition is a narrow vertical stack
in a tall frame, and should instead spread horizontally so the content fills
more width.
